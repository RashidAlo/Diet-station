DietStation Design Lab

Plan card

One design for every plan list, the logged-out home and the checkout plans overlay alike. One source file, copied into each flow, so the two can never drift into two designs again.

The component

Figma Design System 4539-10208, variant A. Rashid, 2026-09-14: “No need to have two different references.” Home’s logged-out plan list is the reference. This specimen renders from system/components/plancard.css and price.css, the same bytes the flows copy.

Anatomy

  1. Platter. “Liquid Glass · Regular · Medium”, painted to match the app’s native glass (v2): radius 32, opaque white, a .75px rim, a white specular inside the top edge, a ~2% interior vignette and an ambient shadow. Press scales to .98.
  2. Meal rows. 18pt count badge (#f2f2f3 with a top highlight, Proxima 600 12) + Urbane 500 12 label, spread over a fixed 122pt column.
  3. Nutrition chip. Hairline pill: kcal | macros, Proxima 12.
  4. Price slot. The Price component, .dsp primary xxxl grad: Urbane 30 numeral and Proxima 15 satellites, cap tops on one line, numeral, KD and decimals painted in the plan gradient, and /mo Medium in #999.
  5. Struck price + strike. The old price in #999 Semibold 15, crossed by Figma Line 79: 1px round-cap #ED1C24 at −9.87°, spanning the price whatever its width.
  6. Deal chip. Hairline pill, Arabic, Avenir Next World 600 12, gradient on the TEXT only (the 🔥 keeps its colour). Optional.
  7. Plan pill. 40 tall, radius 28, the plan gradient with a glass edge (white .5 rim, inner edge glow, top highlight, bottom shade), Urbane 16: Light at 60% + Demi. Press scales to .94.

Before the list, optionally: the offer banner (.lo-offer, white platter, radius 30, monogram 46 at a 28 inset, arrow button 44) and the “or” divider (.lo-or, 95pt hairlines, Proxima 12 #8e8e93).

Themes

One gradient per plan drives three things at once: the price, the deal text and the pill. It is set by data-plan on the card, so a flow never paints a part by hand.

The Diet and LifeStyle are read from the DS component; BODYBuilding and Kids are traced from their instances. BODYBuilding’s pill breaks the two-tone: BODY in white Demi, Building in #F2543D.

Rules

ONE PADDING ON ALL FOUR SIDES. Rashid’s uniform-padding law: nothing sits closer to one edge than another. 20 all round.

IN THE APP THE HOME LIST IS NATIVE GLASS; EVERYWHERE ELSE THE WEB PAINTS ITS LOOK. v2, Rashid on device: Home’s cards (SwiftUI .glassEffect) had depth that Checkout’s flat web cards lacked. Plan cards scroll with content, so they can never be native twins (composition Ruling 4). The web paint layer is sampled from his 3x screenshots (rim 203, top specular, ~2% vignette, ambient 232/220), and is valid on a white sheet only. On colour, re-sample before using it.

THE PRICE IS THE PRICE COMPONENT, NEVER A RE-DRAWN ONE. Home’s first card set the numeral at Figma’s 30/19 line-height. A 19px line box cannot hold 30px Urbane ink, so the gradient numeral was clipped. Figma line-heights do not transfer to CSS, and the cap-top law is what does. Plugging the .dsp slot in fixed it by construction.

  • No deal: leave an empty <span></span> where the chip goes, so the right column keeps its spacing.
  • One-word plan names (Kids) use only the Demi half: <span class="b">Kids</span>.
  • Coupons re-price the card in checkout: only the numbers change, never the layout.

Using it — copy, don’t link

Flow folders are self-contained dev chunks, so a flow never links to /system/. Copy both files into the flow’s folder and link the copies. Line 1 is a version stamp, and everything after it must stay byte-identical to the source.

No local overrides after the link. A rule placed after the copy to change the card is drift in disguise: the diff check passes while the flows diverge. Propose the change to the source instead (that is how v2 came in), then copy it again.

<!-- in the flow's <head>, after its @font-face rules -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="price.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="plancard.css">

# copy (from the repo root)
cp system/components/price.css    <flow>/price.css
cp system/components/plancard.css <flow>/plancard.css

# drift check, run on every Design System change notice: no output = in sync
for f in price plancard; do
  head -1 system/components/$f.css; head -1 <flow>/$f.css
  diff <(tail -n +2 system/components/$f.css) <(tail -n +2 <flow>/$f.css)
done
