Typography

Three voices: Urbane Rounded speaks, Proxima Nova explains, and DietStation Dot performs. Tap any scale row to copy its CSS.

The families

Aa
Urbane Rounded · headings & controls

Every title, button, label and pill. Two cuts only: DemiBold 600 for headings, Medium 500 for controls and labels. Tracking tightens as size grows (-.01em at 19px, -.015em at 30px+). Licensed — served from the lab, never redistributed.

Aa
Proxima Nova · body

All running text: descriptions, notes, metas. Regular 400 only, at a relaxed 1.6 line-height. Falls back to -apple-system so native devices can substitute SF seamlessly.

aa
DietStation Dot · brand display

The dot-matrix brand font compiled from the animation's typewriter grid — four weights (300/400/500/700, weight = dot size), Latin + fully-shaped Arabic, unicase. Display moments only: wordmarks, splash, brand peeks. Never body text.

Rules of voice

Urbane never runs long. If it wraps past two lines it should have been Proxima.
Proxima never shouts. Emphasis inside body text is a color shift to ink, not a weight jump.
Dot never explains. It's the brand performing — one or two words, display size, then hand back to Urbane.
Caps are labels. Uppercase always comes with tracking (+.06 to +.12em) and 500 weight, never larger than 12px.

Dev handoff

Web embed for all three families, and the React Native mapping. RN addresses custom weights by family name — fontWeight is unreliable with custom faces.
