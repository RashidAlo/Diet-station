Rewards — Pull to Redeem

Loyalty coupon sheet with four member tiers, themed 1:1 from Figma. The perforation is punched holes — grab the stub and pull: holes pop one by one with an elastic recoil (haptic tick on supported phones), each ejecting its punched-out disc, until the tab rips free with confetti and the promo code. The nav X is liquid glass on the global recipe.

Theme
Tiered themes the whole sheet per level; Red keeps the brand-red sheet and only the badge reflects the tier (Figma 16842-87694).
User level
Applies that tier's Figma variant — sheet gradient, badge pill, text colors — and re-captures the glass snapshot.
Gesture modifiers
Live — pull a stub to feel each change.
