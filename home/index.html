Home — The Widget System

The DietStation home screen: a modular stack of liquid-glass widgets over brand red. The plan widget wears the subscription's theme; the days-left widget changes shape with the space it's given; hide the promo, discounts or consultation and the neighbours stretch to fill in. Below, today's and tomorrow's meals preview on the white sheet under the floating tab bar.
