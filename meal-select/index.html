Calendar — Your Subscription Days

A live calendar on the real clock: thirty subscription days roll forward from today (Fridays off). Days inside the 72-hour horizon are locked with auto-selected meals — green check; days beyond it show the green plus and open the meal selector empty for manual picking. The first still-open day carries the deadline badge and the header countdown; today is the truck out for delivery, and recent days wear the rating star.

The rule
Real dates, real clock. Days closer than the horizon to their noon delivery lock with auto-selected meals (green check); days beyond it stay a green plus and open the selector empty.
