Calendar — Your Subscription Days

The meal flow now opens on the calendar: every subscription day carries a status chip — green plus means ready to pick, green check is selected, the truck is out for delivery, the yellow star wants a rating, OFF and pause are rest days. Tap any green-plus day and the meal selector rises over the calendar; confirm your plate and the day pops into a check.
