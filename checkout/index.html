Checkout & Payment

The subscription purchase path: pick a plan, choose how long, apply a loyalty coupon, then pay. The coupon button floats over every step — redeeming one re-prices the plan cards, the duration tabs and the total in place, and carries your tier badge through to payment.

Loyalty tier

Which level this member has reached. Tier decides which coupons are unlocked and what badge rides next to the discount.

Entry point

Signed-out shoppers land on the plan cards; a renewing member arrives from the loyalty page straight into their plan's durations.
