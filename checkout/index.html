Checkout & Payment

The subscription purchase path: pick a plan, choose how long, apply a loyalty coupon, then pay. The coupon button floats over every step — redeeming one re-prices the plan cards, the duration tabs and the total in place, and carries your tier badge through to payment.

Loyalty tier

Which level this member has reached. Tier decides which coupons are unlocked and what badge rides next to the discount.

Redeemed coupon

In the product a coupon only arrives one way: the member rips a tab in the rewards flow. This picker stands in for that while reviewing outside the hub — it feeds the same code through the same entry point.

Entry point

From coupons is how checkout opens today: the member rips a coupon in Discounts and lands on the plan cards already discounted at their tier. Signed out starts clean; Renew opens straight into a member's durations.
