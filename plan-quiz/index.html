Plan Builder — Questionnaire

Wireframes for the intelligent diet-plan questionnaire. It opens on the Breathe intro, then asks one question per screen. Three encapsulating directions — Feel lighter, Build up, Stay & thrive — and a health-context checklist right after: gut trouble, pregnancy, or a condition. Checking gut runs the 6-screen naturopath deep-dive (profile, foods, low-FODMAP journey); pregnancy adds a trimester screen and stacks safe adjustments on any goal; a condition attaches a consultation. Every path ends commercially: a drawn 12-week forecast from the user's own numbers, then a duration × meal-count picker with a live calculated price — the chart upsells the longer plan before pricing says a word.
